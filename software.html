Questions

Good to know.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once for the version you buy, per computer.

What does "per computer" mean?

Your licence is a small file made for one computer. It works fully offline and never needs to check in. Replacing a computer? Email us and we'll move your licence across.

What happens when a new version comes out?

Your version keeps working. Bug fixes are free. Big feature updates are optional, for a small fee.

Does it need the internet?

No. It runs fully offline and your data is saved on your computer.

How do I get my licence?

After payment you get an email with the download link, your licence file, an invoice and the licence terms. Nothing is posted.

Does it work on Windows or Mac?

Linux today. Windows is coming soon. Apple Mac is planned for later.

Who builds it?

Josh Goulding, trading as J. Ben G in the UK. Every release is tested, then finalised with AI tools to catch bugs.

Can I buy the source code?

Yes. The full source code buyout is £25,000 under a written agreement. Get in touch.
