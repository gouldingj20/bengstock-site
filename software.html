Questions

Good to know.

What's the difference between Online and Desktop?

Online runs in the cloud, so you sign in on any computer, from anywhere, and pay monthly or yearly. Desktop runs offline on your own Linux computers, with a yearly licence and an offline licence file for each computer. You can buy it now.

How does Online pricing work?

Basic £29.99 a month for 2 users and 5 GB, Pro £59.99 for 4 users and 20 GB, Pro+ £250 for 30 users and 100 GB. Pay yearly and get 2 months free: £299.90, £599.90 or £2,500. Mega is by enquiry, paid yearly, with 500 GB or more.

How does Desktop pricing work?

Basic is £250 a year for 1 computer. Pro is £500 a year for 4 computers. Extra computers are £100 each on either plan. Renew each year to keep your licence active.

Which systems does Desktop run on?

Linux today. Windows is coming soon, and Mac later.

Can I buy the source code?

Yes. The full source code is £25,000 under a written agreement. Enquire.
