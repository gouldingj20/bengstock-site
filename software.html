What BengStock does

One tool, from delivery to invoice.

Everything is grouped by job, so your team finds what they need fast.

Stock

  • Every item in one list: SKU, barcode, location, supplier, cost and sell price.
  • Low and out-of-stock items are flagged, so nothing runs out unnoticed.
  • Plug in a USB barcode scanner and scan to find or book stock.

Goods In

  • Book deliveries in against the purchase order when they arrive.
  • Handle part deliveries and refused goods without guesswork.
  • Stock levels update the moment goods are booked in.

Purchasing

  • Raise, send and track purchase orders to your suppliers.
  • See what to reorder, grouped by supplier, with a suggested quantity and cost.
  • Return goods to suppliers and see which suppliers deliver on time.

Sales, quotes and invoices

  • Write quotes for customers with item, labour and text lines.
  • Turn a won quote into an invoice in one step, and stock is taken out for you.
  • Print quotes and invoices, with your company and VAT details.

Labels

  • Print stock labels with SKU, name, barcode and price.
  • Send them straight to your printer from the Label Maker.

Dashboard and reports

  • The dashboard shows stock value, low items, open orders, and money in and out.
  • Reports for reorder, stock valuation and movement history.
  • Export items and movements to CSV for your spreadsheet or accountant.

Works offline

  • No internet needed. If the broadband drops, BengStock keeps going.
  • Your stock data is stored on your own computers, not someone else's server.

Staff and security

  • Every member of staff signs in with their own login. Add as many users as you need.
  • Every stock change is recorded with who did it, why and when, and can't be quietly edited or deleted.
  • Built-in backups to disk or USB stick, and updates that back up your data first.

Questions

Good to know.

How does pricing work?

Two yearly plans. Basic is £250 a year for 1 computer. Pro is £500 a year for 4 computers. Extra computers are £100 each on either plan. Renew each year to keep your licence active.

How many users and computers can I have?

Users are unlimited on both plans. Computers are set by your plan: 1 on Basic, 4 on Pro, plus any extras at £100 each. Each plan covers the stated number of computers, each running BengStock with its own licence. Shared multi-computer data (server set-up) is coming later. Each computer gets a small licence file made for it. It works fully offline and never needs to check in. Replacing a computer? Email us and we'll move your licence across.

What happens when a new version comes out?

Bug fixes are free while your licence is active. Big feature updates are optional, for a small fee.

Can my staff have their own logins?

Yes. Every user signs in with their own login, and you can add as many users as you need at no extra cost.

Does it need the internet?

No. It runs fully offline and your data is saved on your computer.

How do I get my licence?

After payment you get an email with the download link, your licence file, an invoice and the licence terms. Nothing is posted.

Does it work on Windows or Mac?

Linux today. Windows is coming soon. Apple Mac is planned for later.

Who builds it?

Can I buy the source code?

Yes. The full source code buyout is £25,000 under a written agreement. Get in touch.
