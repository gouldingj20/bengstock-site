Features

Everything you need to run stock

  • Purchase orders

    Raise and track orders with proper PO numbers.

  • Goods received

    Book deliveries in — including part loads and refused goods.

  • Bill of materials

    Build products from components with a clear parts list.

  • QUOTEBOOK

    Turn quotes into invoices in one place.

  • CASHBOOK

    Keep cash pots and movements clear and tidy.

  • Supplier scorecard

    See who delivers on time — and who doesn’t.

  • Works offline

    Your data stays on your machine. No cloud login.

  • Click & keyboard

    Fully clickable, with keyboard shortcuts when you want speed.
