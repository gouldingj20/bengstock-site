About Josh

Why BenGStock

Built because the shop needed it.

J. Ben G started by selling on eBay. Sales were not picking up, and the stock tools on offer did not fit a small workshop. So Josh used his coding knowledge and built BenGStock himself.

It is his idea and his design. He spent the time working out how a small UK shop actually runs, then shaped the screens around that. AI was only a tool in the build. The decisions are his.

Your stock file stays on your own computer. You own the backups. Nobody else hosts your numbers.
