BenGStock · New

Stock control for Linux.

BenGStock covers stock, purchase orders, goods received and cash. It is written for day-to-day business use. The current release is a beta. The licence runs for one year. There is no cloud login.

£199 Yearly licence. Supplied as a Linux .deb. A new key is sent after each renewal payment.

  • One year per payment. A new key is issued after the next payment.
  • Records stay on your computer. You keep the backups.
  • Linux .deb after payment. Windows is in development.
  • Bug fixes for the year covered by the licence.

The key is locked to the machine. When the year ends, the program asks for a new key. Pay again and a new key is sent for the next year. A fault in a listed feature should be reported by email. A corrected .deb is sent within 30 days. If that update does not correct the fault, the remaining licence is ended and the price for that year is refunded. An unlisted feature, or a Windows version, is not a fault.
